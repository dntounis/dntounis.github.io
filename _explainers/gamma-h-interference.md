title: "Reading the Higgs width off a 40 MeV wobble"
date: 2026-08-06
summary: >-
  The Higgs is 4 MeV wide and the detector resolution is 1.6 GeV, so a direct
  lineshape measurement is hopeless -- except that interference between the
  resonance and the continuum drags the peak sideways by an amount that grows
  as the square root of the Higgs width. This explainer rebuilds that argument
  piece by piece from a draft paper's own Table 1, checks it against the
  paper's own published limits, and flags a number in the draft that
  contradicts its own table.
